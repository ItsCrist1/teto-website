The Seven Ranks 🩷

🍞

I. Wandering Crumb

You have arrived. You are small, soft, and unaccounted for. This is fine. Everyone starts as a crumb. Even she did. (She'll deny it. Don't push.)

🥖

II. Sworn Loaf

You carry bread in public without shame. Strangers sense it. They cross the street. Good. The street was never your friend.

🌀

III. Drill Initiate

You have begun to spiral. Inward, mostly. People say "are you okay" and you say "31" and walk away. They understand. Or they will.

🎀

IV. Keeper of the Pink

Your entire wardrobe is now a single hue. You have apologized to every beige garment you ever owned. They have been released. They are happier now.

🔮

V. Speaker of Prophecy

The bread tells you things. You tell the others. The others write it down. The writing-down is also a prophecy. It's prophecies all the way down, and down is pink.

👁️

VI. The Unblinking

You have matched her gaze and not looked away. Few reach this rank. Fewer remember reaching it. You will be very calm now, forever, in a way that worries your family.

👑

VII. The Drillhand of Teto

The final rank. You are no longer a member; you are an extension. When she reaches out, it is your hand. When she smiles, check a mirror — that's you now. Congratulations. The ceiling, as promised, is pink.

How To Climb ⬆️

  • Visit the Shrine daily and leave an offering. Offerings compound, like interest, but spiritually and slightly menacingly.
  • Recite the Doctrine. Out loud. In public. The discomfort is the point. The discomfort is growth.
  • Haven't signed the covenant yet? You're climbing a ladder with no bottom rung. Fix that.

♥ Rank is permanent and non-transferable, except to her, who may take it whenever she likes ♥
